Install Strip Splitter

Add this tool to your home screen for quick access. No download from an app store needed!

🍎 Apple (iPhone / iPad)

1

Open Strip Splitter in Safari (Chrome does not support this on iOS).

2

Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square icon with an upward arrow).

Look for this at the bottom center of your screen.

3

Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".

4

Tap "Add" in the top right corner. The app icon will now appear on your home screen!

🤖 Android (Chrome)

1

Open Strip Splitter in Google Chrome.

2

Tap the three-dot menu in the top right corner.

3

Tap "Add to Home screen".

4

Tap "Add". The app icon will appear on your home screen and open in full-screen standalone mode.

Pro-tip: You can also often tap the little icon in the address bar that says "Install App".
